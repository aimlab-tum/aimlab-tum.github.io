I am a doctoral candidate at the Institute of AI in Medicine (TUM) and the Department of Orthopaedics and Sports Orthopaedics at Klinikum rechts der Isar.
Before starting my PhD, I received an MSc in Computer Science and a BSc in Computer Science: Games Engineering from TUM.
My research focuses on AI-based methods for preoperative planning in orthopaedic surgery, with a particular interest in multimodal AI and medical imaging.
I am always open to discussing topics for Master's theses, IDPs, Guided Research projects, or other student projects.
