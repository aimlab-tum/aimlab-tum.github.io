Stefan is a postdoctoral researcher at the Chair for Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare and Medicine at the Technical University of Munich. His research focuses on efficient tokenization and attention mechanisms for medical imaging, particularly MRI, drawing on tools from applied harmonic analysis. Before joining the chair, he received his PhD in Computer Science from LMU Munich, where he developed explainability methods for image classifiers based on sparse representations.
